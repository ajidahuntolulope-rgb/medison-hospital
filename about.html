About Medison

Built on compassion,
driven by excellence

For over 15 years, Medison Specialist Women's Hospital has stood as a beacon of hope for couples and women across Lagos and beyond.

Our Story

Where it all began

Medison Specialist Women's Hospital was founded on a simple belief: every woman deserves access to world-class reproductive healthcare delivered with warmth and dignity. What started as a single consultation room in Lekki has grown into one of Lagos' most trusted fertility and gynecological centres.

Our affiliation with Harvard Vanguard physicians means our clinical protocols meet international standards. But it's our nurses, our counsellors, and our patient-first philosophy that truly sets us apart.
