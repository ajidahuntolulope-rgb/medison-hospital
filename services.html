Our Services

Care for every stage of life

From routine well-woman checkups to complex laparoscopic surgery, our specialists deliver world-class care tailored to your unique needs.

Service 01

Gynaecological Surgery

Our surgeons are trained in both open and minimally invasive (key-hole) techniques, ensuring faster recovery, less pain, and better outcomes.

  • Endometriosis treatment & excision
  • Hysterectomy (open & laparoscopic)
  • Ectopic pregnancy management
  • Ovarian cyst removal
  • Fibroid removal (myomectomy)
  • Tubal clipping & sterilization
Book a Consultation
Service 02

Hysteroscopic Procedures

Minimally invasive procedures performed through the cervix — no incisions, no scars, rapid recovery.

  • Heavy & painful menstruation treatment
  • Endometrial ablation
  • Fibroid & polyp resection
  • Asherman's syndrome treatment
  • Uterine anomaly repair
Book a Consultation
Service 03

Obstetrics & Antenatal Care

From pre-pregnancy planning to delivery day, we provide continuous, personalised care for you and your baby.

  • Pre-pregnancy care & counselling
  • Early pregnancy consultation
  • Antenatal classes (via Zoom)
  • Growth monitoring & scans
  • Childbirth plan design
  • Normal & assisted vaginal deliveries
  • Elective & emergency cesarean sections
Book a Consultation
Service 04

Well-Woman Care

Preventive healthcare that empowers you to stay ahead of potential issues. Annual checkups, screenings, and honest conversations about your body.

  • Pap smears & cervical cancer screening
  • Breast exams & mammography referral
  • Contraceptive counseling
  • Routine gynecologic screenings
  • Abnormal smear & colposcopy
  • Gynecological ultrasound scan
Book a Consultation
Service 05

Fertility Assessment & Treatment

Comprehensive evaluation and personalised treatment plans for couples and individuals facing fertility challenges.

  • Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) management
  • Recurrent miscarriage treatment
  • Hormonal & ovulation assessment
  • Seminal analysis (partner)
  • Ovarian reserve testing
  • Personalised treatment roadmaps
