The IVF Process

Your journey, step by step

01

Consultation & Assessment

Comprehensive fertility workup including hormonal tests, ultrasound, and semen analysis to create your personalised plan.

02

Ovarian Stimulation

Carefully monitored hormone injections to encourage multiple egg development, with regular scans and blood tests.

03

Egg Retrieval & Fertilisation

Minimally invasive egg collection under sedation, followed by IVF or ICSI fertilisation in our advanced laboratory.

04

Embryo Transfer

The healthiest embryo is gently transferred to your uterus. Any remaining viable embryos can be cryostored for future cycles.

Common Questions

IVF FAQ

A single IVF cycle typically takes 4–6 weeks from the start of ovarian stimulation to embryo transfer. Your body's response to medication may slightly alter this timeline, and we adjust accordingly.

Our overall IVF success rate is approximately 68% for women under 35. Success rates vary based on age, cause of infertility, and embryo quality. During your consultation, we'll provide personalised statistics based on your specific situation.

Most patients describe IVF as uncomfortable rather than painful. Egg retrieval is performed under sedation, so you won't feel anything during the procedure. Mild cramping or bloating after is common and usually resolves within a few days.

We follow international best practices and typically transfer 1–2 embryos depending on your age, embryo quality, and previous cycle history. Our goal is to maximise your chance of pregnancy while minimising the risk of multiple births.

Yes. We understand that fertility treatment is a significant investment. We offer flexible payment plans and can connect you with partner financing options. Speak with our patient coordinator during your consultation.
